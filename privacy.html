Privacy Policy

The Contemporary Austin finance reporting tool

Effective October 14, 2026

What data the tool uses

This application connects to The Contemporary Austin's QuickBooks Online company and reads accounting data for internal reporting.

How the data is used

Data is used only by authorized TCA staff for TCA's financial management. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

Storage and security

Data is stored in systems controlled by TCA, with access limited to authorized staff. Access tokens are stored securely.

Revoking access

The connection can be revoked at any time from the Apps section of QuickBooks Online.
